# Paper bird model sheet

The reference is [the three-quarter view](images/model/three-quarter.png). It shows the only view used in this emoji. A cyan folded head sits above a small, ocean-blue polygonal body. The short legs are near the base and each wing connects at the shoulder. The two eyes on the facial plane are dark dots and the beak is a small orange triangle.

The line is broad and near-navy. The panels are flat with a little color variation from the generated paper. Approximate reference colors are face `#10E3F3`, wings `#008FEB`, outline `#0C143B` and beak `#FF9800`. Keep the fold pattern, face and feet recognizable through the wave. The right wing angle can change while remaining attached at the same shoulder.
