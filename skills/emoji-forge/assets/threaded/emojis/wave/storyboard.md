# Wave storyboard

1. **Opening** • The origami bird faces right, both wings low, eyes open. Hold 430 ms.
2. **Raise the wing** • The wing points across the frame. Keep the body and face steady. Expose for 230 ms.
3. **Hello** • The wing points above the beak. Hold 500 ms.
4. **Settle** • The wing approaches its initial angle. Expose for 260 ms into the cut back to rest.
