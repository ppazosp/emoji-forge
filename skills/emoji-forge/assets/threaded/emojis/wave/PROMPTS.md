## Rest key and style reference · raw/001.png

Create one original character cel for an open-source emoji example. A very simple cute origami bird, ocean-blue and cyan two-tone paper folds, thick near-black navy outline, two small navy dot eyes on the same face plane and one tiny warm orange triangle beak. Front three-quarter view, eyes looking to the right, short legs, both folded wings down. One centered full-body character at about 64% of a square canvas. Crisp flat sticker art, no shadow, no caption, no type, no extra icons or props, transparent background. This cel doubles as the style reference. Keep its silhouette very simple and legible at 128 pixels.

## Peak key · raw/003.png

Reference `raw/001.png`. Edit the supplied origami bird cel into the peak of its wave. Lift only the wing on the viewer's right upwards and outwards at about a 40 degree upward angle, keeping the wing blue with the same navy outline and visibly attached at the same shoulder. The wing tip can be level with the beak or a little higher. Keep all other pixels and features as close to the provided cel as possible: position, size, face and eyes, beak, folds, two feet, viewer-left wing, tail, color palette and black-blue outline. Transparent square canvas, one character, no writing, no motion lines or extras.
