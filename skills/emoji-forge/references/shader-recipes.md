# Shader recipes

Each recipe is a complete fragment you can render, read, remix or combine with your own passes. Use a recipe name in `forge render` or pass the path to any `.frag` file.

## Fire and energy

- [fire](../shaders/fire.frag) — Turbulent flames.
- [plasma](../shaders/plasma.frag) — Looping plasma filaments.
- [lightning](../shaders/lightning.frag) — Forked arcs.
- [aura](../shaders/aura.frag) — Shifting energy around a silhouette.
- [portal](../shaders/portal.frag) — A swirling rim and inner field.

## Lighting

- [bloom-glow](../shaders/bloom-glow.frag) — HDR emission for the bloom pass.
- [god-rays](../shaders/god-rays.frag) — Fanning light shafts.
- [lens-flare](../shaders/lens-flare.frag) — Flare with optical ghosts.
- [neon-rim](../shaders/neon-rim.frag) — Colored light around the silhouette.
- [shine](../shaders/shine.frag) — A traveling band of light.
- [sparkle](../shaders/sparkle.frag) — Glints and stars.

## Impact and movement

- [shockwave](../shaders/shockwave.frag) — Traveling pressure ring.
- [speed-lines](../shaders/speed-lines.frag) — Directional streaks.
- [motion-smear](../shaders/motion-smear.frag) — A trailing smear behind the cel.
- [camera-shake](../shaders/camera-shake.frag) — A brief shake that settles.
- [dust](../shaders/dust.frag) — Ground-level puffs and particles.
- [confetti](../shaders/confetti.frag) — Tumbling strips.

## Weather and liquids

- [steam](../shaders/steam.frag) — Translucent rising wisps.
- [smoke](../shaders/smoke.frag) — A spreading plume.
- [snow](../shaders/snow.frag) — Drifting flakes.
- [rain](../shaders/rain.frag) — Slanted raindrops.
- [bubbles](../shaders/bubbles.frag) — Rising soap rings.

## Distortion and display

- [heat-haze](../shaders/heat-haze.frag) — Refracted art.
- [glitch](../shaders/glitch.frag) — Displaced and split colors.
- [pixel-sort](../shaders/pixel-sort.frag) — Bands of rearranged pixels.
- [crt](../shaders/crt.frag) — Scanlines and curved-glass sampling.
- [black-hole](../shaders/black-hole.frag) — A gravitational lens and accretion ring.
- [hologram](../shaders/hologram.frag) — Flickering lines and cyan light.

## Transitions

- [dissolve](../shaders/dissolve.frag) — Erosion with an emissive edge.
- [shatter](../shaders/shatter.frag) — Displaced tiles of the cel.
- [iris](../shaders/iris.frag) — A circular aperture and rim.

## Depth and perspective

- [beam-burn](../shaders/beam-burn.frag) — A beam and ember trail.
- [beam-perspective](../shaders/beam-perspective.frag) — A widening beam foreshortened toward the viewer.
- [parallax](../shaders/parallax.frag) — Separately moving depth layers.
- [orbit](../shaders/orbit.frag) — Particles that pass behind and in front of the cel.

- [halftone](../shaders/halftone.frag) — Printed dots.
