# Tavvik character bible

Tavvik is a rounded living anvil, happy to help in a workshop. The work surface is the top of the head, with a low tapered horn pointing left, a friendly face inset in the wide front plane, a narrow waist, and a wide pedestal over two stubby boots. Short segmented arms end in shallow outlined mittens. Two soot-dinged brass rims and dark teal lenses sit on top of the anvil. The thick graphite outline carries the silhouette at small sizes.

Keep the horn, waist, pedestal and boot line steady as limbs move. Measure the body independently of the arms, eyes, goggles, and workshop objects. Leave the right of the body clear for props. Sparks belong to the point of contact on the coin or to the effect layer.

## Distinctiveness and small-size review

The name was checked with searches for “Tavvik” alongside anvil, mascot, cartoon and game. The review found no recognizable existing character using that name. The comparison focused on the actual drawn silhouette and features. The horn, tapered waist, wide foot and brass goggles are the cues for this design. The first 32 px render still reads as a metal anvil with a friendly face and leaves the lenses distinct from its eyes. Only original artwork was used as a generation reference.
