# Hammering storyboard

Working on something or celebrating a build.

- **Ready** · The small square steel hammer rests above the workstand, coin on it. Tavvik looks down at the coin.
- **Windup** · The mitten lifts the same hammer high with a slight lean back, feet on the baseline.
- **Impact** · The flat hammer face contacts the gold coin on the workstand. Leave open air for impact effects.
- **Recovery** · The hammer bounces back slightly, then settles into the waiting pose.
