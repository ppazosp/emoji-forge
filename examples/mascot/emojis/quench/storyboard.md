# Quench storyboard

Cool down or mark something done.

- **Ready** · Hold the gold coin above the blue pail in the two-prong tongs.
- **Lower** · Start lowering the coin toward the water.
- **Dunk** · Dip the coin to the waterline and hold.
- **Lift** · Ease the coin back out and smile.
