# Wave storyboard

Say a warm hello.

- **Ready** · Show an easy smile, one mitten at shoulder height.
- **Out** · Move the mitten outward and upward.
- **In** · Move the mitten inward beside the cheek and grin.
- **Ease** · Let the same mitten settle to shoulder height.
