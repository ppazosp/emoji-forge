# Ship storyboard

Send a finished piece to someone.

- **Ready** · Hold a gold disc above the open cedar crate.
- **Drop** · Lower the disc through the opening.
- **Shut** · The gold disc is inside. Close the lid with the mitten.
- **Reset** · The lid bounces ajar as the mitten releases.
