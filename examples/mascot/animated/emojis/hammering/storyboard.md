# hammering

Firing up a build or working on something.

| Beat | Action | Expression | ms |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| Ready | Hammer upright by Ember’s right ear, above a low navy anvil holding a gold disc. | focused eyes | 360 |
| Swing | Hammer swings down halfway, elbow unfolds. Disc and anvil stay still. | focused squint | 120 |
| Strike | Hammer face touches the disc on the anvil. Ember bends the elbow into the strike. | determined squint | 160 |
| Rebound | Hammer bounces just clear of the disc and Ember eases into a smile. | small smile | 280 |
