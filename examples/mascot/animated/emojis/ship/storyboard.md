# ship

Send or deliver something that is ready.

| Beat | Action | Expression | ms |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| Ready | Ember holds a gold smiley disc above a short open cedar crate to its right. | happy smile | 320 |
| Drop | Ember lowers the disc into the crate; the disc remains in its mitten until it reaches the rim. | focused eyes | 150 |
| Shut | Disc is inside. Ember presses the wooden lid closed with a gentle clap. | proud grin | 260 |
| Release | Ember lifts its mitten and the hinged lid bounces ajar, ready for the next parcel. | small smile | 260 |
