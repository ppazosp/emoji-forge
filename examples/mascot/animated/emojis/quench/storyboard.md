# quench

Cool down or call a job finished.

| Beat | Action | Expression | ms |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| Ready | Ember holds a golden puck with tongs just above a short blue water bucket at right. | focused eyes | 340 |
| Lower | The tongs lower the puck to the water line; bucket and feet stay still. | watchful eyes | 130 |
| Dunk | The tongs dip into the bucket so the puck mostly disappears into the water. | squint | 230 |
| Lift | The tongs bring the puck just clear of the water as Ember smiles proudly. | proud grin | 300 |
