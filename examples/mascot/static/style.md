# Ember model bible

Ember is a warm, small workshop fire who likes making things for other people. Front three-quarter is the working view. The turnaround, expressions and hands are filed under [images](images/). The taller middle flame bends left. Two blue goggle lenses sit above a pair of dark oval eyes. A short leather apron covers the lower third, with two short boots below it. Rounded outlined mittens read at emote size. The charcoal outline stays heavier than the apron seams. Two small soot marks are the only intentional surface detail.
